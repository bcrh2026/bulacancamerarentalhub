    Questions
    
    
    Do you rent to first-timers?
    Yes. Bring a valid ID and pay the deposit. We run a short walkthrough on each body so you know the menus before you leave.
    
    
    What if I need the gear for a few hours only?
    The minimum is one day. If you return it the same evening, you still pay one day.
    
    
    Is insurance included?
    No. The deposit covers minor wear. You are responsible for loss or damage, so check whether your own insurance covers rented equipment for the dates you need.
    
    
    Which lens mounts do you stock?
    Each equipment card shows its mount, so you can check a lens fits the camera you are renting.
    
    
    Can you deliver?
    Pickup is at our counter. Ask in your request notes if you need delivery and we will quote it separately.
